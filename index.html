Trust & control

It’s your shop. Mira never goes rogue.

An AI answering as you should make you a little nervous. So Mira is built with guardrails you control.

  • You approve the playbook firstReview and edit Mira’s answers to your top questions before it goes live. Nothing is improvised on price or policy.
  • Money decisions stay with youRefunds, discounts above your limit, and complaints are always handed to you — with context and a drafted reply.
  • Pause it anytime, per channelJumping into a live chat yourself? One tap pauses Mira on that conversation. A big launch coming? Pause everywhere.
  • Every reply is loggedA full transcript of everything Mira said, searchable anytime. Your data is never used to train models for other shops.
